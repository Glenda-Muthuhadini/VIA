# Connected discovery and grade-aware results

Apply v7 once after v6, then v8 once. The owner confirmed v7 plus
`tests/connected_discovery.sql` PASS, and v8 links applied. Historical SQL is preserved;
skip the failed v5 release. The numbered bundles, rather than blindly replaying
every historical migration, are the supported SQL Editor installation sequence.

## Current behavior

- Upload or camera: actual local PDF text extraction / Tesseract OCR reads subjects,
  marks and a grade when unambiguous. No grade question appears before upload.
  Missing or conflicting grade evidence prompts one Grade 9–12 follow-up.
- Manual: choose Grade 9–12, then enter subjects and percentages.
- Detected marks remain editable and require the learner to submit them.
  Extraction never implies that unclear marks have been verified.
- Grade 9/10: academic strengths/focus areas and future study pathways from the
  same catalogue. No APS or university eligibility claim.
- Grade 11/12: supported six-subject APS, excluding Life Orientation, alongside
  mandatory subject checks and NSC qualification gates. This APS is used for the
  supported UJ/UNISA rules; it is not a universal institutional calculation.
- Up to five programmes: exact results first, then one-gap close results when
  space remains. Each close result states its specific missing requirement.
  Requirements are never reduced to manufacture outcomes. Minimums do not
  guarantee admission, and Grade 11 results remain provisional.
- Guest reports stay in browser memory. Existing authenticated private report
  storage is reused. Calculation is read-only and does not overwrite saved marks.

## Catalogue and relationships

58 published records: 10 institutions, 21 programmes, 10 funding opportunities,
17 accommodation listings. Every institution has a programme. Every programme
has a parent institution, requirement information, application information and
funding exploration links. Every funding record connects to programmes.

**11 programmes have complete supported 2027 rules.** The other 10 remain available
for discovery but are excluded from exact eligibility results because their full
rules, scoring system or intake requirements are not supported/verified here.
Unknown dates, prices, distances, NSFAS accreditation and availability remain NULL.
Residence information portfolios are labelled as such; they are not invented
individual buildings or offers. No housing association is invented for UNISA or
False Bay; their detail screens offer general accommodation browsing.

The original normalized catalogue remains the sole source. New provenance-bearing
`via_bursary_programmes` / `via_bursary_institutions` junctions connect funding.
`via_pathway_subjects` maps junior subjects to future study subjects.
`related_public_opportunities` returns existing catalogue documents, not duplicate
records. Public detail dialogs link programme → institution → programmes → funding
and accommodation. Public RLS permits reading published records, never catalogue writes.

Funding links indicate fields/provider scope, not personal awards or eligibility.
ISFAP's UJ relationship is explicitly based on a historical 2024 provider list and
requires current-intake confirmation. Applicant-specific nonacademic criteria are
not calculated. Accommodation links do not assert distance, availability or accreditation.

## Reviewed source inputs

The versioned additions and provenance are in
[`catalogue/connected_catalogue_v7.json`](catalogue/connected_catalogue_v7.json),
generated by `scripts/prepare-connected-catalogue.mjs`. That script refuses to
overwrite changed release inputs. v8 corrects the moved TUT source URLs without
rewriting the released v7 snapshot. All new records use official sources.

New supported options cover UJ public relations, accountancy, accounting and
foundation-phase education, plus UNISA higher certificates in economic/management
sciences, marketing and banking. The diploma accountancy rule distinguishes
Mathematics and Mathematical Literacy APS/mark conditions. NMU's Applicant Score
is deliberately not compared with UJ's APS. Source URLs and verification metadata
are stored with rules, requirements and relationships.

`catalogue/link_verification_v7.json` records the external audit: 45 of 78 URLs
returned successful HTTPS responses; other URLs were blocked or unavailable to
the automated client. Two definite TUT 404s were corrected in v8 using TUT's own
current navigation. This does not certify every external website's availability.

## Verification and repeatable checks

```
node scripts/build-supabase-sql.mjs --check
node scripts/check-qualification-sql-local.mjs
node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/qualification.test.mjs tests/report-results.test.mjs
node scripts/check-connected-discovery-live.mjs
node scripts/check-report-reader-browser.mjs
node scripts/check-discovery-browser.mjs --functional-v6 --checker-only --skip-reader
npm run build
npm run lint
```

The local SQL runner uses disposable PostgreSQL and exercises older releases/tests
before v7/v8, then connected tests. This is separate from the owner's live SQL PASS.
The live runner makes anonymous read-only Supabase calls, checks all catalogue
relationships and realistic Grade 9/10/11/12 results. It never modifies accounts.

Demo profiles include APS 35 Mathematics results, Mathematics 66% with high APS
(which cannot bypass a 70% requirement), APS 24 Mathematical Literacy with English
45% (three certificate results and two English 50% close results), and APS 19
certificate pathways. Grade 9/10 profiles produce future pathways only.

The browser and anonymous live checks passed after the v7/v8 installation.
Build and seven qualification/parser unit tests passed. Lint reports only the
existing landing-page `Date` render warning; no landing code was changed here.
All historical SQL bundles match their migration sources, and all local SQL tests passed.

The browser runner uses synthetic reports with actual OCR/PDF extraction and
real anonymous Supabase queries. It covers unknown-grade follow-up, manual input,
junior/senior results, exact/close explanations, connected detail navigation,
method switching, malformed files, guest upload privacy and widths 375, 430, 768,
1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920, plus short mobile/laptop heights. It does not certify
every possible school report format or a physical device's camera hardware.
